Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about renovation costs, permits, and timelines in Singapore.

How much does an HDB BTO renovation cost in Singapore?

When it comes to renovation costs in Singapore, there is no one-size-fits-all number. Because every home is unique, the final investment amount depends entirely on your specific requirements, the exact scope of works, and your preferred design direction & budgets.

What is the difference between a direct carpentry contractor and an interior designer?

Interior design (ID) firms focus primarily on 3D drawings, concept styling, and sub-contract the fabrication with markups. Direct carpentry contractors own their workshop and build cabinets in-house. ReHoVa combines design consultation with direct workshop fabrication, giving you complete design support without middleman markups.

How is custom carpentry priced in Singapore (pfr)?

Carpentry is priced per foot run (pfr). In Singapore, standard full-height wardrobes average $280 to $380 pfr, kitchen base cabinets average $150 to $200 pfr, and top cabinets average $130 to $180 pfr. Table tops to be priced separately. Pricing varies depending on selection of laminate, internal carcasses, and hardware fittings.

How long does a renovation take from key collection to handover?

A standard HDB BTO renovation takes 6 to 8 weeks after permit approval. Resale flats or private condos requiring hacking, wet works, and rewiring take 8 to 12 weeks. We map out an exact week-by-week timeline before work starts on site.

Do you handle HDB and condo MCST renovation permits?

Yes. All hacking, electrical, and plumbing works require formal submissions. We handle the HDB renovation permits, BCA compliance, and condo MCST renovation permits on your behalf before commencing work.

Can I walk in to see material samples at your Ubi showroom?

Yes. Our showroom is located at 215, 217 & 219 Ubi Ave 4, Intrepid Warehouse Complex, Singapore 408810. You can review laminate samples, stone countertops, and test working hardware in person. Booking an appointment ensures a designer is available to review your floor plan without waiting.
