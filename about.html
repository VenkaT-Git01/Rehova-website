About Us

Interior Design & Renovation

ReHoVa Interiors Pte Ltd, UEN. 201711139N, our associate company, aspires to help you realise your vision of building a functionally and aesthetically more pleasing environment and space.

ReHoVa Interiors Pte Ltd, is an experienced renovation contractor that specialises in Residential and Commercial full renovation and fitting out works. We are well-equipped with a dedicated and dynamic team of experienced project managers, supervisors, designers, carpenters and construction workers to provide quality workmanship and value-for-money solutions for your renovation needs.

We have an in-house carpentry workshop in Singapore to complement our renovation works to achieving a shorter project turnaround time at lower cost to benefit our clients. We believe in using good quality building materials for our renovation works so that our clients enjoy minimum future maintenance costs. With our excellent track record of many successful projects and satisfied long term clients, we have the confidence and the ability to deliver all your renovation needs to your satisfaction.

Our designers have many years of experience and they are knowledgeable to understand the realm of your ideas and ideals. They will do the necessary research, conceptualise and incorporate your ideas and ideals where possible, into the design for our renovation team to build your dream space.

The name, ReHoVa carries our commitment to proudly proclaim that our brand stands for Re = Reliable, Ho = Honest, and Va = Value for Money which together serves to remind our renovation team to stay Reliable, Honest and to provide Value for Money for our clients.
