Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers on renovation pricing, carpentry workmanship, permits, and showroom visits.

How much does an HDB BTO renovation cost in Singapore? +

A standard 4-room HDB BTO renovation in Singapore generally costs between $38,000 and $65,000. Basic essential work with standard vinyl flooring, painting, and modular kitchen carpentry starts around $30,000 to $40,000. Full makeovers with custom feature walls, full-height wardrobes, and wet work range from $50,000 to $70,000+.

What is the difference between a direct carpentry contractor and an interior designer? +

Interior design (ID) firms focus primarily on 3D drawings, concept styling, and sub-contract the fabrication with markups. Direct carpentry contractors own their workshop and build cabinets in-house. ReHoVa combines design consultation with direct workshop fabrication, giving you complete design support without middleman markups.

How is custom carpentry priced in Singapore (pfr)? +

Carpentry is priced per foot run (pfr). In Singapore, standard full-height wardrobes average $240 to $320 pfr, kitchen base cabinets average $120 to $160 pfr, and top cabinets average $110 to $150 pfr. Pricing varies depending on laminate brands, internal carcasses, and Blum hardware fittings.

How long does a renovation take from key collection to handover? +

A standard HDB BTO renovation takes 6 to 8 weeks after permit approval. Resale flats or private condos requiring hacking, wet works, and rewiring take 8 to 12 weeks. We map out an exact week-by-week timeline before work starts on site.

Do you handle HDB and condo MCST renovation permits? +

Yes. All structural hacking, electrical, and plumbing works require formal submissions. We handle the HDB renovation permits, BCA compliance, and condo MCST security deposit guidelines on your behalf before commencing work.
