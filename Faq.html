Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers on renovation costs, carpentry workmanship, permits, and showroom appointments in Singapore.

Do I need to book an appointment before visiting the Ubi showroom? +

Walk-ins are welcome at our Ubi showroom (217 Ubi Ave 4), but booking an appointment beforehand is recommended. Scheduling ahead ensures an interior designer is available to review your floor plan, provide material samples, and discuss quotations without waiting.

How much does an HDB BTO or condo renovation cost in Singapore? +

Renovation costs depend on your unit size, masonry requirements, and custom carpentry volume. A standard 4-room BTO flat typically ranges from $35,000 to $65,000, while resale flats and private condos vary based on hacking and plumbing needs. With our direct workshop, we provide transparent pricing without middleman markups.

Do you have your own direct carpentry workshop in Singapore? +

Yes. ReHoVa Interiors operates direct carpentry production. This allows us to maintain strict quality control over joinery, Blum soft-close mechanics, and laminate finishes while keeping fabrication lead times predictable.

How long does a home renovation usually take? +

A new HDB BTO renovation generally takes 6 to 8 weeks upon permit approval. Resale flats requiring hacking, rewiring, and wet works typically take 8 to 12 weeks. We provide a milestone-tracked renovation schedule before works begin.

Do you handle HDB and BCA renovation permits? +

Yes. We manage all statutory submissions, including HDB renovation permits, BCA compliance, and condo MCST working permits on your behalf.
